Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 25, 2026 | Application: Ledgerly (Package: com.aistudio.expensetracker.mvkqxl)

Your privacy and financial confidentiality are paramount to us at Ledgerly. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application and related web services handle, store, and protect your information.

1. Information We Collect

Ledgerly is designed with an offline-first architecture to minimize the collection of personal identifying data:

  • Financial Records & Transactions: Transaction amounts, custom category tags, dates, descriptions, recurring intervals, and forecast milestones created within the app.
  • Authentication Data: If you choose to authenticate via Google Sign-In, we receive your email address and display name to associate your synced Firestore database records with your account. If you choose Guest Mode, no identity data is transmitted.
  • AI Prompts: When utilizing natural language expense logging, the short text prompt you submit is sent securely via TLS to Google's Gemini API strictly for JSON extraction and parsing.

2. How Your Data Is Stored

  • On-Device Local Storage (Room SQLite): All transactions, recurring rules, forecast pipelines, and brainstorming notes are saved directly on your Android device's private application sandbox.
  • Cloud Synchronization (Firebase Firestore): For authenticated users, your encrypted financial data is synchronized in real-time across your authorized devices using Google Cloud Firebase security rules.

3. Third-Party Services

Ledgerly integrates with reputable, enterprise-grade cloud services that maintain their own strict privacy standards:

  • Google Play Services & Firebase: For user authentication, cloud database sync, and crash analytics.
  • Google Gemini API: For natural language parsing of user-entered financial summaries. We do not use your financial data to train public AI models.

4. Data Retention & Deletion

You maintain complete ownership and control over your financial records:

  • You can delete individual transactions, recurring series, forecast entries, or custom categories directly in the app at any time.
  • To delete your entire account and all cloud Firestore records, you can execute a data purge within the app settings or submit a request to our support email.

5. Security Safeguards

All communications between the Ledgerly application and cloud endpoints (Firebase Firestore and Gemini API) are encrypted using industry-standard Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3).
