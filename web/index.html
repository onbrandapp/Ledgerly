Key Features

Designed for complete financial mastery, from instant natural language parsing to multi-month pipeline planning.

⚡
Feature 1

1. Smart AI Natural Language Logging

Powered by Google Gemini 2.5 Flash

Type or paste natural phrases to log expenses and revenue in seconds:

"Spent $14.50 on lunch at Chipotle" "Received $2,400 monthly freelance retainer from Acme Corp" "Paid $68 for electric utility bill"

Gemini intelligently extracts the financial amount, category, type (Expense/Income), and description in structured JSON format and pre-fills the entry sheet with a single tap.

Click "Parse" or click any example chip above to test live...
📋
Feature 2

2. Complete Financial Ledger & Search

Comprehensive bookkeeping and transaction management built for speed and clarity.

  • Full transaction history with instant search by note, memo, or merchant.
  • Smart Filters: Filter by date range (Today, This Week, This Month, Custom Date Spans), transaction type (Expense, Income, All), or Category.
  • Instant CSV export and data summary totals for easy tax reporting.
  • Paid / Unpaid Status: Mark transactions as Paid or Unpaid to reconcile bills and pending receipts.
📈
Feature 3

3. Future Income Forecasting & Pipeline Manager

Project upcoming invoices, anticipated client retainers, year-end bonuses, and expected dividends with active milestone management.

  • Pipeline Status: Track and sort projected income by Confirmed, Expected, or Tentative.
  • One-Tap Realization: Convert forecasted revenue directly into active ledger income once funds are received.
  • Interactive Milestones: Attach bulleted checklists to forecasts with completion toggles and strikethroughs.

💡
Feature 4

4. Brainstorming Notes & Scratchpad

Capture and incubate revenue opportunities before they turn into contracts or active projects.

  • Bulleted Notes: Dedicated spaces for future income ideas, rate increase goals, and side hustle milestones.
  • Custom Color-Coded Tags: Organize concepts with customizable category tags.
  • Interactive Checklists: Step-by-step progress tracking with real-time completion checkmarks.
🔄
Feature 5

5. Automated Recurring Transactions

Put predictable income streams and subscription bills on total autopilot.

  • Flexible Cadences: Create recurring rules on Daily, Weekly, Monthly, or Yearly frequencies.
  • Subscriptions & Bills: Perfect for rent, cloud hosting, SaaS subscriptions, utilities, and retainer invoices.
  • Smart Automation: Auto-logs entries with background verification, notification controls, and active series management.
📊
Feature 6

6. Budgeting & Visual Analytics

Keep cash flow healthy with proactive visual targets and real-time breakdowns.

  • Monthly Spending Targets: Dynamic progress indicators with color-coded warning thresholds.
  • Interactive Visual Charts: Dynamic expense breakdowns by category and multi-month cash-flow trend charts.
  • Personalized Categories: Create custom categories with custom color palettes and vector icons.
🛡️
Feature 7

7. Dual Persistence: Offline-First + Cloud Sync

Your financial data is always private, ultra-fast, and safely backed up.

  • Local Database (Offline-First): Zero-latency, completely offline functionality powered by Jetpack Room (SQLite). Works anytime, anywhere.
  • Cloud Database (Sync): Real-time cross-device synchronization with Firebase Firestore.
  • Secure Authentication: Seamless Google Identity sign-in protection or completely private guest mode.
